Privacy Policy - Bangla Keyboard

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Bangla Keyboard ("the app") is a Bangla phonetic and English keyboard for Android, published by Infomity ("we", "us").

Summary

The app works entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal data or anything you type.

What the app does with what you type

  • Everything you type is processed locally on your device, only to convert it to Bangla and suggest words.
  • Typed text, suggestions, and the words you pick are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no internet permission and makes no network connections. It cannot send data off your device.
  • Words you pick from suggestions are remembered only in the device's memory while the keyboard is running. They are not written to storage, and are discarded when the keyboard service stops.
  • In password, e-mail, and URL fields the app switches to a plain English layout and does not offer suggestions.

Data stored on your device

The app saves only three settings, in private app storage: whether Bangla digits are shown, whether key vibration is on, and your last language mode (Bangla or English). This stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Automatic cloud backup of app data is disabled.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics, or crash reports. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking code.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions beyond the standard ability to act as a keyboard (input method) that you enable yourself in Android settings.

Word list

Suggestions come from a word list bundled inside the app, so no lookups happen online. The word list is derived from the openly licensed (CC0) AI4Bharat IndicCorp v2 Bengali corpus; only derived word counts are bundled, not the original text.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
