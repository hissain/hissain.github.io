Privacy Policy — Bangla Keyboard

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Application: Bangla Keyboard Package: com.infomity.banglakeyboard Publisher: Infomity

Summary

Bangla Keyboard works entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal data, keystrokes, credentials, or anything you type. The application requests zero network permissions and makes zero internet connections.

1. What the App Does with What You Type

  • Everything you type is processed purely locally on your device hardware, solely to convert input characters into Bangla script and display word suggestions.
  • Typed text, suggestions, and the words you pick are never sent to the publisher or any third party.
  • The app does not have the Android android.permission.INTERNET permission and makes no network connections whatsoever. It is technically incapable of sending data off your device.
  • Words you select from suggestions are remembered only in volatile device memory while the keyboard service is actively running. They are not written to persistent storage and are cleared when the keyboard service stops.

2. Sensitive Input Fields (Passwords, Emails, URLs)

In password, e-mail, and URL input fields, the app automatically switches to a standard English layout. Word suggestion caching and auto-corrections are completely disabled in these fields to guarantee privacy.

3. Operating System Permissions

Bangla Keyboard requests no special or dangerous runtime permissions. It requires only standard Input Method permissions:

  • Input Method Service: Standard capability required by Android to act as an on-screen keyboard.
  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide optional haptic feedback when you tap a key. This can be toggled off in settings.

4. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The application saves only basic user preferences locally in private app storage:

  • Whether Bangla digits are enabled
  • Whether key haptic vibration is active
  • Your last selected language mode (Bangla or English)

This data remains strictly on your device and is permanently removed if you uninstall the app. Automatic cloud backups of application data are explicitly disabled.

5. Offline Dictionary & Word List

All word suggestions originate from a pre-packaged, offline word frequency list bundled directly inside the APK. No dictionary lookups ever occur online. The word list is derived from the openly licensed (CC0) AI4Bharat IndicCorp v2 Bengali corpus; only statistical word counts are bundled, not the source texts.

6. Information We Collect

None. We do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (names, emails, phone numbers)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, MAC address)
  • Location information
  • Contacts, files, or photos
  • Analytics, telemetry, or crash reports

The application contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no third-party tracking code.

7. Children's Privacy

Bangla Keyboard does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.
