PRIVACY POLICY — BANGLA KEYBOARD
=================================
Last Updated: November 9, 2026

Application: Bangla Keyboard
Package Name: com.infomity.banglakeyboard
Publisher: Infomity
URL: https://hissain.github.io/android/bangla-keyboard/privacy/


SUMMARY
-------
Bangla Keyboard works entirely on your device. It does not collect,
transmit, or share your personal data, keystrokes, credentials, or anything
you type. The few things it keeps (your settings and the suggestions you
picked) stay in private storage on your device. The application requests zero
network permissions and makes zero internet connections.


1. WHAT THE APP DOES WITH WHAT YOU TYPE
---------------------------------------
- Everything you type is processed purely locally on your device hardware,
  solely to convert input characters into Bangla script and display word
  suggestions.
- Typed text, suggestions, and the words you pick are never sent to the
  publisher or any third party.
- The app does not have the Android android.permission.INTERNET permission and
  makes no network connections whatsoever. It is technically incapable of
  sending data off your device.
- Words you pick from the suggestion strip are remembered on your device so
  the same choice is offered first next time (up to 500 entries). They are
  kept only in the app's private storage, are never sent anywhere, and can be
  erased at any time with "Reset learned words" in the app's Setup screen.
  Words you merely type are not recorded. Next-word suggestions come from a
  word-pair list bundled with the app; nothing is learned from your typing for
  them.


2. SENSITIVE INPUT FIELDS (PASSWORDS, EMAILS, URLS)
---------------------------------------------------
In password, e-mail, and URL input fields, the app automatically switches to
a standard English layout. Word suggestion caching and auto-corrections are
completely disabled in these fields to guarantee privacy.


3. OPERATING SYSTEM PERMISSIONS
-------------------------------
Bangla Keyboard requests no special or dangerous runtime permissions. It
requires only standard Input Method permissions:
- Input Method Service: Standard capability required by Android to act as an
  on-screen keyboard.
- Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide optional haptic
  feedback when you tap a key. This can be toggled off in settings.


4. DATA STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE
-------------------------------------
The application saves only the following, in private app storage on your
device:
- Your settings: number row, Bangla digits, key vibration, key sound, key
  preview bubble, next-word suggestions, double-space full stop, keyboard
  theme (system, light or dark) and key height
- Your last selected language mode (Bangla or English)
- Your recently used emoji (up to 24)
- The suggestions you picked (up to 500 entries), which can be erased with
  "Reset learned words" in the Setup screen

This data remains strictly on your device and is permanently removed if you
uninstall the app. Automatic cloud backups of application data are explicitly
disabled.


5. OFFLINE DICTIONARY & WORD LIST
---------------------------------
All word suggestions originate from a pre-packaged, offline word frequency list
bundled directly inside the APK. No dictionary lookups ever occur online. The
word list is derived from the openly licensed (CC0) AI4Bharat IndicCorp v2
Bengali corpus; only statistical word counts are bundled, not the source texts.
The next-word suggestions use a small word-pair list derived from the same
corpus and bundled in the same way; the keyboard does not learn word pairs
from what you type.


6. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
-------------------------
None. We do not collect:
- Personal identifiers (names, emails, phone numbers)
- Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, MAC address)
- Location information
- Contacts, files, or photos
- Analytics, telemetry, or crash reports

The application contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no
third-party tracking code.


7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
---------------------
Bangla Keyboard does not collect any personal information from any user,
including children under the age of 13.
