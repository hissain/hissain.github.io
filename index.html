Engineering Leadership & Vision

Currently, I lead the Health Sensing Modalities Team at Samsung, pioneering AI-driven physiological sensing and on-device edge intelligence. Key innovations include real-time stress detection algorithms for smart earbuds, non-invasive electrochemical sweat sensors (in partnership with Caltech), and personalized circadian phase (DLMO) estimation models (with MIT).

My work also encompasses training on-device foundation models for continuous blood pressure monitoring, heart rate variability (HRV), and executing clinical trials on women's health. My technical domain spans Digital Health, Mobile Systems, Distributed Architecture, Low-Power BLE, and Digital Signal Processing (DSP).

Beyond system architecture, I explore AI, physics, philosophy, and music—viewing software engineering as a vehicle to address challenging physical-world problems and enhance human well-being.
